## Auto-fixes triggered in this run

Phase 8 ran an independent review in a fresh subagent context, with no visibility of how the copy was produced. It returned one FAIL and several notes. All were fixed in-run rather than deferred.

1. **FAIL, AI-tell cadence (Standing Rule 9).** Zero em dashes, but the sentence *constructions* were dense enough to read machine-written: 17 not-X-but-Y reversals (four consecutive opening paragraphs each built on one), 6 escalating triples including a four-part anaphora repeated verbatim in both the blog and the LinkedIn caption, and one stacked-fragment pair reskinned across three parent captions. **Fixed:** blog body rewritten, reversals now 0 against a budget of 3, the repeated anaphora rewritten as a plain list, and the fragment pair re-derived separately for Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.
2. **Note, zero contractions anywhere.** Student voice with no contractions is both off-register for a 15-16 year old and an independent AI tell. The house voice samples use them. **Fixed:** contractions restored across the blog and the TikTok caption.
3. **Note, unsupported quantified claim.** The lede, index excerpt, TikTok, X and Pinterest all asserted 'about thirty hours' saved, but the arithmetic shown in the post only derived five and a half. **Fixed:** the figure was removed everywhere rather than inflated, and replaced with 'removes most of the friction from the year ahead'. No caption now carries a bare statistic, so no `Source:` line is required.
4. **Note, factual.** '39 week school year' overstates Year 11, which loses several weeks to study leave. **Fixed:** changed to roughly 33 teaching weeks, and the derived figure adjusted from six and a half hours to five and a half.
5. **Note, Rule 4 coverage.** The blog made a spec-mapping claim without the exam-board disclaimer, which only the LinkedIn caption carried. **Fixed:** disclaimer added to the blog body.
6. **H2 count.** First draft had 8 H2 sections against a 5 to 7 spec. **Fixed:** the closing summary heading became a bold lead-in. Now 7.
