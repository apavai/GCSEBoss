## New live-API finding, logged to DECISIONS.md

Pinterest rejected the first attempt with HTTP 400: `Pinterest title is too long (485 characters). Maximum allowed is 100.` Pinterest has two separate text fields, `title` capped at 100 and `description` capped at 500, and `post_photo()` maps its caption argument to `title`. Every other platform takes the full caption as `title`, so Pinterest is the single exception in the routing table. Retried with the pin title as the caption and the body passed as `description`, which succeeded. The parameter is `description`, not `pinterest_description`. Written up in `DECISIONS.md` as an amendment to the 24 August Pinterest entry.
