## Seasonal mode

Results day plus six days. Enrolment is at its peak and the course-change window is open right now. Practical anchor for the post: most providers handle a swap at enrolment itself, then through September, and many allow a move up to October half term or Christmas of Year 12 if the timetable blocks permit. That gives a real answer to "how late is too late". Two constraints decide almost every swap and are worth naming plainly: whether the class has space, and whether the two subjects sit in the same block. Search intent peaks now to mid September, then falls off sharply.
