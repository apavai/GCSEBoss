Coverage

Every Board. Every Subject.

GCSE Boss covers all 174 specifications across the five UK exam boards. Whether your school teaches AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC/Eduqas, or CCEA — every specification point is mapped, every topic broken down. One of the very few revision apps to support all five.

See every subject by board →

AQA
Assessment and Qualifications Alliance

England's most-used GCSE exam board. Strongest coverage across English Language, English Literature, Mathematics, the Science suite, History, and Geography. Used by around half of England's schools.

Edexcel
Pearson Edexcel

The UK's largest commercial exam board, owned by Pearson. Broad reach across Mathematics, the Sciences, Modern Foreign Languages, and Business Studies. Specifications updated regularly to match curriculum changes.

OCR
Oxford, Cambridge and RSA Examinations

Part of Cambridge University Press & Assessment. Particularly strong in the Sciences, Computer Science, Classical Civilisation, Latin, and Religious Studies. A favourite among grammar and independent schools.

WJEC
/Eduqas
Welsh Joint Education Committee

Wales's main exam board, with Eduqas-branded specifications also widely used by schools in England. Particularly strong in Religious Studies, Design & Technology, Drama, and Performing Arts.

CCEA
Council for the Curriculum, Examinations and Assessment

Northern Ireland's exam board, the statutory body of the Department of Education. Distinct subject set including NI-specific content alongside the standard GCSE suite — and we cover all of it.

GCSE Boss is not affiliated with or endorsed by any exam board. We map their published specifications so students can revise exactly what's in their syllabus.
