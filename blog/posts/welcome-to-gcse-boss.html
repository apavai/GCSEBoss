Welcome to GCSE Boss

We're building GCSE revision around the specification your school actually teaches — not a generic question bank that happens to mention GCSE.

Most revision apps treat GCSEs as one big subject. Biology is biology. Maths is maths. But if you're sitting AQA Combined Science while your friend is on Edexcel Separate Sciences, you're not revising the same thing — even when the topic names look similar.

GCSE Boss starts from your exam board's published specification. Every spec point is mapped. Every topic is tied to what your syllabus actually requires. That means when you revise Cell Structure, you're revising your Cell Structure — not a mash-up of content from three different boards.

Built for how GCSEs actually work

We cover all five UK exam boards — AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC/Eduqas, and CCEA — across 174 GCSE specifications and over 9,000 individual spec points. Whether your school uses the most common board in England or a Welsh or Northern Irish specification, the app is structured around what you're actually being examined on.

Premium features include AI explanations in plain English, unlimited homework scans with worked solutions, offline revision sessions, and advanced analytics that show exactly which spec points still need work.

For parents, not just students

GCSE revision shouldn't be a black box. Premium includes a parent dashboard for up to two adults on one account — live activity, missed session alerts, and subject coverage broken down by topic. You see what's happening without having to ask every evening.

Coming soon to the App Store

GCSE Boss is iPhone-only for launch. We're putting the finishing touches on the app now and will share the App Store link here as soon as it's live.

This blog is where we'll post revision tips, product updates, and parent guides as we go. If you have a question or topic you'd like us to cover, get in touch.
