Year 11 Homework: What Changes in the First Fortnight

Year 11 homework is not last year's colour-the-poster job with a new cover. It is a shorter, sharper piece that only works if you leave the room with the task in your bag, not in your head.

Some of you sat in lessons yesterday. Some of you are walking in today. Some of you still have INSET or a staggered start until Thursday. None of that puts you behind. The first fortnight of Year 11 homework starts when you actually have a piece to do, not when a national calendar says term began.

The change is not volume. It is what the teacher expects you to already know, and how little they will repeat at the door.

What year 11 homework looks like in week one

Last year a lot of homework was finish the worksheet, copy the diagram, colour the keywords. You could do it with the book open and one eye on a video.

Year 11 homework is closer to this: finish the example they started, then do two more without looking. Or: write the method, then one question that uses it. Or: read a page and answer three questions that are not copied from the page.

That is why it feels heavier even when it is shorter.

Teachers will still set forms and logins in week one. Those count. Bring them back. Do not treat a signed sheet as a wasted evening. The first real exercise books start once you have sat in the lesson. If you have not sat in it yet, you have no homework from that subject. Wait.

Do not copy a friend's list from a group chat as your plan. Their school may already be on period six. Yours might still be finding rooms.

Write the task down before you leave the room

This is the whole skill for the first fortnight.

Before you stand up, write:

  • the subject
  • the exact task in one sentence
  • where it lives (book, sheet, classroom platform)
  • when it is due
  • how long they said it should take

If they did not say a time, guess 25 minutes and stop when the timer ends. You can ask tomorrow. You cannot reconstruct a question at 9pm from "something about photosynthesis".

Phones are in bags or lockers in a lot of English schools this term. That is a school rule, not a national one. If you cannot photograph the board, the written line in the planner is the whole record. Do not rely on a photo you were not allowed to take.

If you missed the last two minutes because you were packing, stay 30 seconds and ask. "What exactly do I need to hand in?" is a normal question. It is not a confession.

How long year 11 homework should take

A first-fortnight piece is usually one sitting. Twenty to forty minutes for one subject is a reasonable default unless the teacher said otherwise. Two hours on one worksheet is not dedication. It is a sign you do not have the question, or you are revising the whole topic instead of doing the piece.

Set a timer. Do the task. Stop.

If you finish early, you are done. Do not pad it with extra pages because the evening feels empty. If the timer ends and you are stuck, write the last line you understood and one sentence about where it broke. That is useful for the teacher. A blank page is not.

Stacking five subjects into one night in week one is how people end up awake at eleven with nothing finished. Pick the due-soonest piece. Do that. Park the rest for tomorrow. You are not building a two-year timetable this week. You are clearing what was actually set.

Year 11 homework is not revision

This is the mix-up that wrecks September.

Homework is the task from that lesson. Revision is going back over a topic you have already been taught, on purpose, without a due date from today.

If you sit down to "do chemistry" and open last year's notes, you are not doing tonight's homework. You are avoiding it. Close the extra tabs. Find the question. Do that.

You do not need a colour-coded year plan in the first fortnight. You need a list of what was set, with due dates. One page. Phone note or paper. Cross it off. That is a year 11 homework timetable for September. You can add a proper revision slot later, once you know which topics this teacher actually started with. When you do get to that, start from the spec, not a generic list.

Mocks are not this week's job. May is not this week's job. The useful test is: can you explain tonight's method tomorrow without the book? If yes, the piece did its work. If no, that is what you ask in the next lesson.

When to message the teacher

Message if you physically do not have the question. The platform is down, the sheet was not given out, you were in music for that period. One short message: subject, date, what is missing. Send it that evening, not at 11pm, and not the morning it is due.

Do not message because the question is hard. Hard is the point of Year 11 homework. Bring the stuck sentence to the next lesson.

Do not message to negotiate the due date on day two. If there is a clash (fixture, family thing, you were not in), say that once, early, with the date. Then do the bit you can.

Parents can help you find the platform login. They cannot reconstruct the question. If they start a hallway quiz about every subject, you can say you will show them the planner after food. That is fair. The work is yours. The evening does not need a briefing.

A first-fortnight setup that actually holds

Keep the kit boring.

Planner or phone note with today's tasks. Timer. The book or sheet in the bag, not on the bedroom floor. One quiet surface. Kitchen table is fine. You do not need a new desk for week one.

One sitting per piece. If you have three pieces, that is three sittings, not a five-hour block. Eat first if you are hungry. Then start. A skipped meal plus a blank worksheet is a bad combination and it is not a character test.

If a club or a job means you get in late, do the shortest piece tonight and park the longer one for tomorrow. Write that down.

What you can ignore for a fortnight: panic workbooks, colour-coding every subject, rewriting unused notes, and any speech about how this year matters more. Leave with the task, do it once, bring it back.

Tomorrow morning is the check

Before you leave, look at the planner. Is the piece in the bag? Is the form signed? That is the whole check.

If you were not in today, you still do not need a homemade homework list. Wait until you have a lesson. You are not behind a national clock.

Year 11 homework gets easier to live with once you stop treating it as a vibe and start treating it as a sentence you wrote before you stood up. Write the task. Time-box it. Hand it in. Then you can have the evening.

That is the first fortnight. The rest of the year has time.
